Three-Pod Architecture

One ankle hub.
Two muscle nodes.

Each athlete wears three devices simultaneously. The two Muscle Pods stream raw EMG over ESP-NOW to the Ankle Pod hub, which fuses neuromuscular timing data with three-axis kinematics and relays a composite signal to the coaching iPad over BLE.

No setup beyond applying the disposable electrode patches, snapping the pod on, and securing the ankle strap.

Ankle Pod — Hub
Receives muscle data from both muscle pods. Transmits graphs and real-time data to coach app via Bluetooth.
Muscle Pod × 2 — Nodes
Four-electrode colinear array with magnetic snap attachment. Tracks muscle signal activity and transmits to Ankle Pod for algorithm calculations.
Flex Patch — Disposable
Single-use electrode flex patches array. Uses medical adhesive material and sweat-resistant. One per session.

What MackRun Measures

The signals
that matter.

Every metric MackRun generates is a direct physiological indicator — not a proxy, not an inference from accelerometer noise. Real neuromuscular data, captured at clinical resolution, during live practice.

CV m/s
Conduction Velocity
Motor unit firing speed along the muscle fiber. Decreases measurably with fatigue before any performance decline is visible externally.
PAI ms
Pre-Activation Index
Time between EMG onset and the corresponding ankle acceleration event. Quantifies neuromuscular readiness — a primary Achilles injury risk marker.
MF Hz
Median Frequency
EMG spectral centroid. Shifts leftward with muscle fatigue. Tracked per-channel across both the gastrocnemius and tibialis anterior.
6°-axis
Ankle Kinematics
3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyroscope at 416Hz. Gait pattern, stride symmetry, impact force estimation, and joint loading.
L/R Δ%
Bilateral Asymmetry
EMG amplitude and timing differences between the medial gastrocnemius and tibialis anterior. Compensation patterns appear before pain does.
RTP score
Return-to-Play Score
Composite neuromuscular readiness index derived from conduction velocity, pre-activation index, and bilateral symmetry. Objective clearance data.
Game-Day Workflow

Sixty seconds to data.

01 — PREP
Patch application
Equipment staff applies disposable flex patches to target muscles. Peel, press, done. No conductive gel, no skin prep beyond a quick wipe.
02 — ATTACH
Pod snap-on
Rigid pod snaps magnetically onto the flex patch. N52 magnets self-align the unit. Pogo pins make contact. System boots automatically on motion detection.
03 — MONITOR
Live coaching view
Coach iPad connects over BLE. Real-time fatigue index, asymmetry flags, and conduction velocity trends update per stride. Actionable during practice.
04 — RECOVER
Post-session analysis
Pods return to the dock. Flex patches are discarded. Full session data syncs to the platform. Return-to-play scores generated overnight.

Why MackRun Exists

Achilles ruptures end careers.
The signal that predicts them
has always existed — in the muscle itself.

We built MackRun to make muscle fiber conduction velocity measurable during live athletic activity for the first time. Every component — from the 24-bit analog front end to the disposable electrode patch to the coach-facing application — was designed around one question: what does the athlete's nervous system know that the coaching staff doesn't?
