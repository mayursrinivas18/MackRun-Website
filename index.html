Most of rehab happens where no one can see it

Patients spend a few hours a week in the clinic and the rest of the week on their own.

Whether they wore the boot, did their heel raises, or pushed too hard on a good day usually comes down to what they remember at the next appointment. Progress decisions rest on those snapshots.

MackRun fills in the days between. It measures what the tendon is actually doing at home, so you and your patient can make the next decision with the whole week in view.

How it works

  1. Wear it

    The patient straps the pod on just above the ankle. It knows when it's being worn and runs for days between charges.

  2. It syncs on its own

    Whenever the pod is near the patient's phone, the app collects the day's results and uploads them. No buttons to press.

  3. Review and adjust

    Your dashboard shows each patient's wear time, exercises and loading. Update their program, and it reaches their app.

What it measures

Everything is summarized into plain daily numbers for you and your patient. Detailed recordings from exercise sessions are there when you want to look closer.

  • Wear time

    Hours worn each day, detected through skin contact, temperature and movement, so a day of "no activity" is never confused with a day the pod sat on the dresser.

  • Exercise sessions

    Sessions completed and repetitions counted for exercises such as heel raises, compared with what you prescribed.

  • Walking and activity

    Steps, active minutes and walking patterns across the whole day, not just during exercises.

  • Landing impact

    How hard each landing is as patients return to hopping and running, recorded in detail even on the hardest landings.

  • Skin temperature

    The daily temperature trend over the lower leg, compared with the patient's own baseline, so unusual changes stand out for you to review.

  • Left and right

    With a pod on each leg, see how the recovering side compares with the other, one of the clearest signals for return-to-activity decisions.

Questions

Is MackRun a medical device?

MackRun is in development and has not been cleared by the FDA. It isn't intended to diagnose or treat any condition. It gives clinicians information to support their own judgment.

Who is it for right now?

Physical therapy clinics treating Achilles injuries and tendinopathy, and their patients during rehab. During development, MackRun is available through partner clinics.

How long does the battery last?

We're designing for several days of everyday wear per charge. Pilot units will confirm real-world figures, and the app warns patients before the battery runs low.

What does the patient need to do?

Wear the pod, keep their phone nearby now and then, and charge it. Syncing happens on its own.

What happens to the data?

Daily summaries go from the pod to the patient's app and on to the clinic's dashboard. Detailed session recordings are collected when the pod is docked at the clinic. Data is only shared with the patient's own clinic.

What does a pilot involve?

Pods for your patients, a charging dock and a tablet for your clinic, onboarding with our team, and regular check-ins to hear what's working and what isn't.
